Common questions

Is this the official Fantasy Premier League app?
No. FPL SquadPilot is an independent, unofficial assistant. It reads public Fantasy Premier League data but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League.

Can this app change my Fantasy Premier League team?
No. The App is read-only — it never asks for your Fantasy Premier League login and cannot make picks, transfers, or any other change on your behalf.

How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings → Delete my local data in the App. That clears everything the App has saved on your device, including your data choices. If you have had anonymous usage analytics switched on, turning it off in Settings → Your data choices also resets the identifier behind it. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and what is collected.

How do I get rid of the adverts?
Buy premium. The free version of the App is paid for in part by a single banner advert, so there is no switch to turn it off — a premium entitlement removes it. Adverts are never personalised.

Can I turn off usage analytics?
Yes, any time, in Settings → Your data choices. Nothing in the App is withheld if you do. In the UK, EU, EEA and Switzerland nothing is collected unless you agree in the first place, and you can reopen that choice from Settings → Ad privacy options.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google, not by us. Cancel from your App Store or Google Play account's subscription settings.

Why do points/projections look different from the official app?
Live points can lag briefly around bonus points and defensive contributions until Fantasy Premier League finalises a gameweek. Projections are estimates, not the official app's numbers.
