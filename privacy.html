Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

FPL SquadPilot is published by baliddeki. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

FPL SquadPilot has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. It reads public Fantasy Premier League data and stores your preferences on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

What the app stores on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your FPL team IDTo show your squad, points and league tablesStays on your device
Appearance preference (light / dark / system)To remember how you like the app to lookStays on your device

Both values are held in the operating system's standard local preference store. Neither is transmitted to us. You can erase both at any time from Settings → Delete my local data.

Your FPL team ID is a public identifier, not a credential. FPL SquadPilot never asks for your Fantasy Premier League email address, password, or session, and cannot make changes to your FPL team.

What the app sends over the network

The app requests public, read-only endpoints on https://fantasy.premierleague.com. These requests include your team ID when you have connected one, because that is how the public API returns your squad and league tables. They contain no other information about you.

The Premier League operates those servers and its own privacy practices apply to them. We have no control over, and no access to, their logs.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No accounts, so nothing to delete on our side.

Subscriptions

FPL SquadPilot offers optional paid features via Apple's App Store and Google Play's in-app billing. Payment is handled entirely by Apple/Google; we never see or store your payment details. Subscription management, cancellation, and refunds are handled through your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children

FPL SquadPilot is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL, with a new "last updated" date.
